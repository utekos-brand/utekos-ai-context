# Connectors in Canva AI

Bring your app into Canva AI.

Connectors in Canva AI let Canva users access their tools — like Gmail, Notion, and Google Calendar — right inside Canva AI. We're building out our connector ecosystem and want to hear from companies whose products could be a great fit.

## About Connectors in Canva AI



AI Connectors are currently read-only, and let Canva AI pull in real content from a user's connected tools. A user might ask Canva AI to turn their latest emails into a presentation, or build a project update from their calendar — Connectors make that possible without ever leaving Canva.

* **Current scope:** Read-only access only, text content only (no images or
  videos).
* **Technical standard:** MCP (Model Context Protocol).

<Note>
  We're not currently accepting submissions for connectors developed by third-parties. Right now, Canva builds and maintains all AI Connectors. We're working toward opening this up, but we don't have a timeline to share. Submitting interest here doesn't guarantee your product will be built or listed.
</Note>

## What we're looking for

We're focused on tools that Canva's users already rely on and where connecting to Canva AI would meaningfully improve their workflow. Strong candidates tend to:

* Have broad adoption among creative, marketing, and business teams.
* Expose data that's genuinely useful as context for design and communication tasks.
* Already have or are actively building an MCP server.
* Have a technical contact who can move quickly when we're ready to build.

## Express your interest

Fill out our [Interest form](https://forms.gle/Sw2Wypnsb4NCbsbq8). We review submissions regularly and will reach out if there's a potential fit.

## Other ways to be in Canva AI

To integrate your tools or services with Canva AI sooner, explore the following intents for apps inside Canva:

* [Data Connector](https://www.canva.dev/docs/apps/intents/data-connector/) — enables read-access to tabular data.
* [URL Expander](https://www.canva.dev/docs/apps/intents/url-expander/) — lets users enter URLs into Canva AI to automatically pull in content.
