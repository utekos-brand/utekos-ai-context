# Canva concepts

The Canva Admin API helps you manage teams, groups, and users within a Canva organization. This article describes the Canva organizational concepts that are relevant to using the Admin API.

## Teams, groups, and users

Canva users are organized into *organizations*, *teams*, and *groups*. For [Canva Enterprise](https://www.canva.com/enterprise/) customers, an **Organization** is the largest organizational unit, and can contain many teams, groups, and users. For non-enterprise customers, a **Team** is the largest organizational unit.



All users are in *at least* one team; including [Canva Free and Canva Pro users](https://www.canva.com/pricing/), who are in their own (individual) teams. Users in a team have access to all content shared with that team and can easily [share content with other team members](https://www.canva.com/help/team-sharing/).

Groups belong to *a* team and can't be shared between two teams. Users don't need to be part of a group, but they can be in one or more groups.
